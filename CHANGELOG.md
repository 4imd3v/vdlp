# Changelog

The section of a version here is the release notes the update window shows.

## 0.2.1

- A Subtitles page: paste a video or a playlist and VDLP downloads the
  subtitles of it, or of every video in it, into the downloads folder.
- A download can embed the subtitles in the video, in the languages you
  name, from the New download page.
- The subtitles are fetched as the srt YouTube keeps one line to a cue, and
  rewritten into blocks of the two lines a player draws, so they stay in one
  place instead of moving up as each line arrives.

## 0.2.0

- VDLP updates itself: it checks for a new version when it starts and once a
  day, shows what changed, and installs it, then relaunches. "Check for
  Updates…" in the Help menu asks now.
- The About page shows the version, and has the same check.
